Watch and Be Ready

Heading 1: Watch and Be Ready Do not be deceived, we are living in the last days. We do not know the day nor the hour but we can know the season. We are warned to be vigilant and watchful for Jesus’ return. The best example of this is the parable of the 10 virgins where 5 are ready and 5 are not. The five that are ready, enter the wedding and the other five are not allowed in. We are told in verse 13 to “Watch therefore, for you know neither the day nor the hour in which the Son of Man is coming.” We must be ready when Jesus comes.
