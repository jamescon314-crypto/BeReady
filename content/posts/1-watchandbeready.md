## Do Not be Deceived

Do not be deceived, we are living in the last days. We do not know the
day nor the hour but we can know the season. We are warned to be
vigilant and watchful for Jesus’ return. The best example of this is the
parable of the 10 virgins where 5 are ready and 5 are not. The five that
are ready, enter the wedding and the other five are not allowed in. We
are told in verse 13 to “Watch therefore, for you know neither the day
nor the hour in which the Son of Man is coming.” **We must be ready when
Jesus comes**. To be otherwise will result in dire consequences, to be
left out.

## Questions to Ponder

How will Jesus find me when he comes, being watchful and doing his will
or distracted by the world?

Which of the five best describes me, one of the five that are ready or
one of the five that are not?

Do I believe that Jesus could come at any time, even tonight?
